Weeks 1–12

Content, live and at volume

First content goes live in weeks 1–2, then publishes continuously at your cadence. We lead with the formats AI engines cite most: direct answers, comparisons, category roundups and clear definitions, with schema and your AI instruction page in from the first piece.

How an article gets made

  • Research agent pulls keyword data, competitor content and current news.
  • Writer agent drafts it for its destination: blog or newsletter.
  • Editor agent scores it for SEO, AEO and GEO and revises until it passes. Nothing below the bar ships.
  • Our editors review every piece before it ships. Your job is to brief us, not to edit.
  • Publisher agent pushes it to your CMS by API, and we submit it for indexing.

Your inputs shape every article

  • Tone and voice guide: how every article sounds.
  • Brand guidelines: naming, claims, formatting, visuals.
  • Audience and ICP: who it speaks to, and the decision they're making.
  • Do-not-use and do-not-contact lists: words, people and companies we never use.
  • Compliance standards: required disclaimers and allowed claims, checked against every draft.

The protocol

Agreed in writing before work starts

AI answers vary by session, location and login, so every run follows the same rules and every run is evidenced.

1

What counts

Your brand in the answer, or your domain in the source panel. A third-party page mentioning you doesn't count unless the answer names or links you.

2

Clean room

Logged out, fresh session, personalization and memory off, fixed locale, default model, query pasted verbatim, no follow-ups.

3

Three runs, majority rule

Each query runs three times per engine. 2 of 3 counts as cited; 1 of 3 is logged as partial.

4

Evidence for every run

Timestamped screenshot plus raw answer text in your shared folder. If it's not in the folder, it's not in the report.

5

No answer isn't a miss

If an engine gives no AI answer for a query, it's excluded from that query's result. Written into the agreement.

6

Verify it yourself

Re-run any query under the protocol. Disagreements are settled by a joint re-run within five business days of the Day 90 report.
