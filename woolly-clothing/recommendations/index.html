15. AI visibility, measured across five engines

A 5-engine probe across 16 buyer prompts, Aug 10, 2026.

Average share of voice: ~25%. The composition is the finding.

Every prompt Woolly wins is a branded prompt: "Woolly Clothing reviews," "Is Woolly Clothing true to size?", "Are Woolly leggings squat proof?", "Woolly bundle and save deals," "Woolly vs Ridge Merino," "MerinoAire vs Everyday Boxer Brief."

Every unbranded category prompt is a miss. "Best merino wool boxer briefs for men," "Where to buy affordable merino wool base layers," "Is merino wool underwear worth it for hiking?", "Best 4-season merino wool tops for travel," "Why is merino wool better for odor control?", "What are the benefits of merino wool vs synthetic activewear?" - absent from 4 of 4 engines on every one.

Woolly is cited when someone already knows the name. Exactly the pattern the 5.91% branded versus 0.18% non-branded CTR shows in classic search. The same disease on two surfaces.

The paradox: Woolly scores 5/5 on AI-crawler readiness and roughly 1/5 on AI-citation outcome. They opened the door and put nothing quotable behind it.

Why competitors win

  1. Comparison content. Dedicated "vs" pages. Woolly has none.
  2. Editorial affiliate footprint. Smartwool appears in roughly 80% of "best of" listicles.
  3. Certifications in headers. Competitors put Woolmark, RWS and OEKO-TEX in H1s. Woolly buries the same certs in an FAQ accordion and a product badge.
  4. Dated, crawlable content. Icebreaker and Smartwool publish weekly. Woolly has 3 posts and 939 words.

20a. The ICP and the content gap point at the same four keywords. The secondary ICP is sustainability-motivated, and plastic free clothing, eco friendly activewear, natural fiber activewear and sustainable underwear brands are all open goals nobody ranks for. Your tagline is literally the keyword. That alignment is unusual and should be the first content sprint.

20b. Ridge Merino is the real competitive threat, not Icebreaker. Same affordable-merino position, and they appear in AI answers where Woolly does not. Woolly vs Ridge Merino is the one comparison prompt Woolly currently wins, which makes it the one to defend and the model for the rest.

20c. The tertiary CTA points at the broken page. Your own "Learn more" call-to-action sends buyers to /pages/merino-magic - 250 words, no H1, and a placeholder meta description. The designed customer journey terminates at the weakest page on the site.

22. Recommended course of action

Phase 0 - Diagnose and stop the bleeding. Week 1, ~3 days.

  • Find out what shipped between June 1 and 5. Theme and app change log. Highest-value hour in the engagement.
  • Replace the merino-magic placeholder. Sweep for others.
  • Root-file check: RESOLVED. llms.txt, agents.md, robots.txt and a clean 195-URL sitemap are all confirmed present. The agentic-readiness story is real.
  • Confirm which robots-blocked and noindexed pages are intentional Shopify defaults before drawing any conclusion from the raw indexation number.
  • Fix the content-page meta template, add H1s, add product image alt text, put the blog in navigation.

Phase 1 - Capture the demand you already have. Weeks 2 to 4.

This is where the money is, and a conventional SEO engagement would do it third.

  • Restore the 46 lost product snippets.
  • Ship the merchant-listing fields: hasMerchantReturnPolicy, shippingDetails, description, global identifiers. 1,350 items, cheapest high-leverage fix available, and it puts the one-year guarantee and free shipping directly into the SERP.
  • Fix the review-count caching bug, then ship AggregateRating so star ratings appear.
  • Rewrite titles and metas across the top 50 URLs by impression volume, not by traffic.
  • Redirect audit on the 444 hard 404s and the -copy URLs. Do not rename anything before this completes.
  • Consolidate the four underwear URLs onto /collections/underwear, the one Google is already choosing.
  • Parameter handling to kill the com_cvv index bloat.

Phase 2 - Take the open goals and the category term. Month 2.

  • Four pages for the four uncontested keywords: eco friendly activewear (720/mo), plastic free clothing (590), natural fiber activewear (480), sustainable underwear brands (320). ~2,110/mo combined, nobody ranking, nobody cited, directly aligned to the secondary ICP. Cheapest rankings in this document.
  • Entity hardening: Wikidata entry, LinkedIn company page, Crunchbase profile, on-site press page. Disambiguate from Wool&.
  • merino wool underwear (9,900/mo) from 11.15 onto page one. Aggregate the merino education content that already exists into a pillar page. Wire up Okendo events so review work becomes measurable. Add structured-data monitoring so the next June 3 is caught in 48 hours.

Phase 3 - Own the category conversation. Month 3+.

Comparison pages against the named competitor set, Ridge Merino first (direct positional threat), then Smartwool and Icebreaker. The merino wool t-shirts (6,600/mo) authority play, built on the henley page's existing 885,854 impressions. Press page, reviews hub, dated cadence. AI visibility monitoring with the GA4 AI Assistant channel as the revenue proof. Address the closeout-page dominance so discovery stops running through the discount door.

The separate conversation

Organic Social delivers five times the new users of Organic Search at 28% of the value per user. That is a bigger lever than anything technical in this document, it is not an SEO decision, and we do not know what that channel costs you to run. We raise it as an observation rather than a recommendation.
