The pattern

Two sites in one.

Someone built your product pages properly. The layer above them was never finished. That single split explains why you convert well and rank nowhere.

Built right

  • llms.txt and agents.md served, ahead of the category
  • Google Knowledge Panel present, organic position 1 on brand
  • Unique meta descriptions and correct canonicals on every product page
  • Real spec depth: 175 GSM, 17.5 micron, 100% Australian merino
  • 1,350 valid Merchant Center items at position 1.53
  • RWS certificate 1313848, Woolmark, OEKO-TEX, BSCI
  • /collections/underwear at position 2.5, growing 13%

Never finished

  • Two placeholder pages live and indexed
  • One identical meta description across every content page
  • 93 collections for 68 products, 23 of them internal or operational
  • Roughly 850 URLs Google has tried and cannot use
  • Best sellers on URLs ending -back-up-copy
  • Four URLs competing for one search, so three of them lose
  • Blog is 3 posts, 939 words, linked from nowhere
  • No LinkedIn, Wikidata or Crunchbase entity off-site

Your two underscore underwear collections are down 34% and 42% over three months. The clean, hyphen-free one is up 13% and sits at position 2.5 with four times the site-average click-through. Google has already decided which URL it prefers. We should agree with it.

The plan

Hygiene first, so the growth work compounds.

Publishing new content onto a template that serves one duplicate description to every URL wastes the content. Sequence matters more than volume here. The work bench has all 76 tickets with sizes, owners and dependencies.

Week 1

Diagnose and stop the bleeding

Find out what shipped between June 1 and 5. Replace both placeholders. Fix the content-page description template, add the missing H1s and product image alt text, and put the blog into navigation. Roughly three days.

Weeks 2 to 4

Capture the demand you already have

Restore the 46 lost product snippets. Ship the missing Shopping fields across 1,350 items. Fix the review-count caching, then add rating markup. Rewrite titles and descriptions by impression volume. Redirect audit on the 404s and the duplicate product URLs. Consolidate the underwear collections.

Month 2

Take the open goals and the category term

Four pages for the four uncontested keywords. Move merino wool underwear from 11.15 onto page one. Aggregate your existing merino specs into a page that can rank. Harden the off-site entity. Add structured-data monitoring so the next June 3rd is caught in 48 hours.

Month 3+

Own the category conversation

Comparison pages against your named competitors, Ridge Merino first. The merino wool t-shirts authority play. Press page, reviews hub, dated publishing cadence, and AI visibility monitoring across all five engines.
