What Claude thinks you are

"What is Ombrik?" (asked of the AIs)

Ombrik is a managed service provider for go-to-market engineering, founded by Ronit Yawalkar out of Dallas (office in Houston) and spun out of his CRM product Conversion Crate in 2025. Instead of a tool, you get an embedded team: GTM engineers plus a CRO/RevOps lead who own your tool stack, integrations, AI-powered ABM, and revenue operations end to end. "The GTM Engineering team you've been trying to hire," and "Not another tool. A better system." The pitch is work with us instead of hiring a $250K in-house GTM engineer you can't find. It's working: 50+ clients, only 4 churned, concentrated in consulting and IT services.

Here's the disconnect. Your foundation is strong: a clear, well-defined niche entity, real schema, a fast site, brand-name clients like TreasuryPath, and near-zero churn. But when we asked the AIs the questions your buyers actually ask (best GTM engineering agency for IT services, outsourced RevOps for consulting firms, and so on), Ombrik never came back. The answers went to RevPartners, Go Nimbly, ColdIQ, Belkins and Hinge. An independent probe (DataForSEO, Sept 12, 16 prompts across 5 engines) found the same: cited just 2 of 80 times. The reason is simple and fixable: you publish nothing. Zero blog posts, so there is literally nothing for Google or the LLMs to rank or quote, plus no llms.txt and no comparison pages. And "GTM engineering" is a category being born, so almost nobody owns these answers yet. Whoever publishes first becomes the default answer.
