Deep dive

Maximizer Mike, in his own words.

"I'm 41 years old, and I make a comfortable living, probably a little higher than most homeowners. I'm tech-savvy and spend quite a bit of time online... I consider our home our most important investment, so I make an effort to research and educate myself on how best to take care of it. I'm not the kind of person who delays repairs or improvements... The more I do, the more confident I become as a homeowner."

Messaging principles · the how

How to write for Mike.

Be active and actionable

Meet his readiness with language that feels active and messages that are actionable.

Make it feel effortless

Lean into Hippo's tech-native identity and the ease and convenience of the platform, since Mike expects seamless digital experiences.

Frame the long-term value

Show how Hippo protects a home's value long-term and boosts ROI. Proactive protection and tailored policy options both support this.

Empower with education

Be the partner who boosts his confidence with educational content and resources.

Key themes · the what

Five themes to draw from.

Internal guidelines for crafting copy, not lines to publish verbatim.

Ahead of every risk, behind every home

+

Hippo gives people the information and resources to prevent incidents and avoid claims before they happen.

Why it matters to Mike

He isn't a procrastinator. He values being an informed, hands-on homeowner and is actively looking to improve and protect his home.

Why it matters to Hippo

This is our core differentiator and master value prop for choosing Hippo over other insurance options.

  • Fewer incidents. Fewer claims. Less worry. Protection that's designed to be proactive, so you can feel confidently in charge.
  • Clean up after a kitchen remodel. Not after water damage.
  • Others wait for claims. We're in the business of preventing them.
  • A dream home means improvements instead of fixes.
  • Protect your home. And your peace of mind.

Smarter approach

+

Hippo is a tech-native platform led by insurance experts, offering fast, accurate quotes and add-ons beyond the standard policy, the opposite of "set it and forget it" insurance.

Why it matters to Mike

He uses tech across every part of his life and expects, and pays for, seamless experiences.

Why it matters to Hippo

Distances Hippo from the slow, legacy-monolith perception of insurance and positions the brand as agile and tech-enabled.

  • Home insurance that's built for today's homeowners. Policy options from top carriers in 60 seconds or less.
  • Get quotes from 70+ carriers, instantly.
  • Your partner for everything home insurance.
  • Built by insurance experts. For today's homeowners.

Your home, your way

+

People can choose the policy that works best for them, including add-ons, based on their unique needs.

Why it matters to Mike

He's a confident homeowner who wants choices and control, and has the resources for add-ons.

Why it matters to Hippo

Lets us speak to different homeowner situations and emphasize the breadth of Hippo's offerings.

  • Insurance options for every kind of home. From a one-bedroom bachelor pad to a six-bedroom family home, find the policy that fits your needs.
  • Your home, your way. Instantly get home insurance quotes from over 70 carriers, tailored to you.
  • We deliver the options. You choose your policy.

Protecting your home's value, now and in the future

+

Investing in home insurance and home care with Hippo is a long-term play in preserving the value of the home.

Why it matters to Mike

He sees his home as his most valuable asset and is looking for the best ways to protect that investment.

Why it matters to Hippo

Stresses the big-picture value of home insurance as a positive ROI driver.

  • Make sure your most valuable asset stays that way.
  • Protect your home today. Increase its value tomorrow.
  • We're playing the long game. Protect the value of your home long after you've paid off your mortgage.
  • From the first time to a lifetime.

A partner for first-time homeowners

+

The resources and insurance a first-time homeowner needs to protect their investment.

Why it matters to Mike

He's likely a first-time homeowner (75% of this persona is) but isn't lacking in confidence. He wants resources, tools, and a partner.

Why it matters to Hippo

Speaks directly to the first-time homeowner demo and wins their trust.

  • First-time homeowners choose Hippo. Educational resources. Seasonal home maintenance checklists. Insurance experts. We have you covered.
  • First home jitters shouldn't be about insurance.
  • New homeowner? No problem.
  • First time homeowners, meet lifelong expertise.
