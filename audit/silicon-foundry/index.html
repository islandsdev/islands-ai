Quick glossary

AEO
Answer engine optimization: shaping content so AI assistants quote it when they answer.
GEO
Generative engine optimization: the same idea, aimed at ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini.
AI Overview
The AI summary Google shows above its regular results.
llms.txt
An optional plain-text guide to a website for AI agents. Google says it doesn’t need one.
