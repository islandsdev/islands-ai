"What is Radical?" — asked of the AIs

Radical is a pre-launch intimates startup building custom-fit, wire-free push-up and strapless bras that are modeled from a 3D scan of your body taken with your phone — its line is "The Future of Fit." The founding team cites backgrounds from OpenAI, Apple, Meta, Stanford and Invisalign, positioning the product as engineering-led rather than traditional fashion. Four of the five tools, Claude included, could not connect your category to your name. Only ChatGPT surfaced you, and only on the single "3D body scan" question.
