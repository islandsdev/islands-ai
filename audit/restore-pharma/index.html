What We Found

Five findings. Restore is winning the answers that existing patients ask, losing every answer a new patient asks, and sitting on clinical proof that no engine can currently quote.

Getting cited by AIBehind

Who AI thinks you areBehind
4 leaders, 0 readable
The homepage says Restore was formed with over 50 years of combined experience by Canadian pharmaceutical leaders. All four appear as photographs with no crawlable bio text, no Person markup and no links out. Off site there is no Wikipedia entry, no Wikidata record, no Crunchbase profile and no press section.
Asked "How to book a consultation with Restore Pharmaceuticals," the engines pulled from Restore Health Consulting, RestoreHealthROC and RestoreFM. Asked whether Restore is trusted by professional athletes, the cited sources included a funeral home and two Instagram accounts.
Two quick fixes we found while crawling: the pharmacy phone number appears only inside a homepage FAQ accordion, so both the crawl and Google's knowledge graph missed it, and the footer's Instagram and TikTok links still point at Shopify's own accounts rather than Restore's.
