How we measure ROI

From a published article to revenue

Every article we publish should earn its place in Google and in AI answers, bring visits, and turn some of those visits into leads. Here is the math we use, the funnel we drive, and the 15 steps behind it.

In short

We have catalogued 2,771 published articles, and the typical article we publish is first shown in Google within 3.5 days. We measure the first three stages of the funnel every week. Leads depend on your conversion tracking, and revenue on your close rate and deal value, so we tell you exactly which numbers are live and which are waiting on you.

The ROI math

What our articles are worth, against what you pay us

Two kinds of value. Traffic value is what the same clicks would cost you as Google Ads. Lead value uses your close rate and deal size.

Traffic value=clicks on our articles × Google Ads cost per click
Lead value=leads from our articles × close rate × average deal value
ROI=traffic value + lead value − what you pay uswhat you pay us

Traffic value is an estimate: Google hides the search term behind most clicks, so we price them at the median cost per click of the searches we can see.

Behind the scenes

The 15 steps that produce these numbers

Each step has one owner and one output you can see. Here they are, with the real files behind them.

Steps 1–4Day Zero: measure where you stand before we write

  1. 01
    Research your market and competitors.

    We crawl your site, map your competitors and pull real search demand into one workbook, your Venture Input Pack.

  2. 02
    Score your visibility on 19 signals.

    Each signal across search (SEO), AI-readiness (GEO) and AI answers (AEO) gets a score from 0 to 5. The average is your Day Zero score.

  3. 03
    Ask AI assistants what your buyers ask.

    16 real buyer questions go to ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Google AI. We record whether they cite you, and who they recommend instead.

  4. 04
    Record your starting point.

    Week one of your KPI sheet becomes the Start column. It never changes, so every later week is compared with it.

Steps 5–8Build: write, publish and link

  1. 05
    Plan the calendar from real demand.

    Our topic agent turns the gaps from steps 2 and 3 into topics with keywords, funnel stage and planned links. No topic without a demand signal.

  2. 06
    Write and check every article.

    About 20 specialist AI steps write, link, edit and score each draft against your voice, audience and legal rules. An Islands editor signs it off.

  3. 07
    Publish and tell Google.

    We publish to your CMS and submit the new page to Google, which starts the clock in the funnel above.

  4. 08
    Link it in, and check the links daily.

    Each article links to your other articles and to related brands, and earns links back. A daily check confirms every link is still live; the counts appear under “Links we built” on your KPI sheet.

  1. 09

  2. 10
    Re-ask the buyer questions every month.

    Track visits from search and AI assistants.

    Organic and AI-assistant visits, and how engaged they are.

  4. 12
    Count leads from the articles.

    Your site's conversion events, plus tracking codes on our calls to action, tie each lead to the article it came from.

  5. 13
    Turn leads into revenue.

    Your close rate and average deal value turn article leads into a revenue estimate in the ROI math. No lead lists or CRM access needed.

What we guarantee

  • We credit only our own work: articles our system wrote, published after we started.
  • Every number names its source and date.
  • A person reads and sends every weekly story.

Limits worth knowing

  • A few articles bring most of the clicks, so we name the winners rather than quote an average.
  • Leads need conversion tracking, and the revenue figure is an estimate built on your close rate and deal value.
  • AI citations move slowly, so we read them monthly.
  • Search Console data arrives 2–3 days late.
