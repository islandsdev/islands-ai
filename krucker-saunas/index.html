What We'd Build

Content is the front door. The engine is what happens after someone knocks.

Publishing alone leaves money on the table. The full motion is four parts: content placed where sauna buyers actually decide, agents that answer every inbound within minutes, an outbound motion into the B2B buyers nobody in your category is calling, and a market split so each buyer hears the pitch built for them.

Content built to convert, and placed
Placed, not posted
Every piece is built as a conversion surface first. The permit guide ends in a barrel sauna quote, the cedar comparison ends in a model picker, the winter piece ends in a delivery enquiry. Then the same asset gets placed off your domain: the Ontario Made listing, Sudbury and cottage-country directories, the wellness and cottage communities where these decisions actually get discussed, and cut into the short-video formats your Instagram and TikTok already perform in.
The three homepage films and the gallery are the raw material. One shoot you have already paid for becomes ten placed assets.
What it drives
A published post earns a citation. A placed post earns a conversation. Same writing cost, and placement is where the AI citation and the human referral both come from.
How it runs
Writing and brand-editor agents draft in your voice. Channel agents reformat per platform. The backlink agent finds and builds the off-site placements.
Inbound agents on every channel
Minutes, not days
You have five social channels, a financing application, a quote cart, a contact form and a dealer page. Every one is a live lead source with no automated first response. An inbound agent replies to DMs, comments, form fills and financing enquiries within minutes, asks the two or three questions you actually need answered (site access, electric or wood, timeline), and books the call.
Speed to first reply is the largest controllable variable in a $4,999 to $8,499 considered purchase. The demand for this already exists, which is why it pays first.
What it drives
Same traffic, more booked calls. This is the fastest revenue in the plan because it converts interest you are already generating and currently losing to response time.
How it runs
Reply agents across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, email and the site, handing straight to a human the moment a build gets specific or a number is on the table.
Outbound into the B2B side nobody works
B2C and B2B
Everything you publish today points at homeowners. The identical product sells to resorts and glamping operators, cottage rental managers, spas and wellness studios, gyms and recovery studios, and the builders and landscape designers who specify outdoor amenities. You already have a Dealer Locator page, which means the channel exists and is not being fed.
A resort or rental operator buys four units, not one, buys again, and does not stop buying in February.
What it drives
Bigger orders, repeat orders, and revenue that is far less seasonal than backyard demand in Ontario.
How it runs
Vertical target lists built from real operator data, sequences written off the same content so the outreach carries proof rather than a pitch, and outbound agents that handle the replies and book.
Verticals and new markets
5 slices
Muskoka and remote cottage owners, small-yard urban buyers in Toronto and Ottawa, custom configuration buyers, Ontario hospitality and short-term rental operators, and the dealer channel. Each gets its own content, its own sequence and its own landing page instead of one message aimed at everyone.
"We Go Anywhere" is a positioning line today. Sliced by market it becomes five different pitches, each with its own proof.
What it drives
You already know the first three segments well. The last two are sitting completely unworked, and they are the ones with order sizes above one unit.
How it runs
The strategy agent maps each slice. The reporting agent tells us within weeks which slice is actually paying, and we move effort there rather than guessing.
