The two formats AI lifts fromBehind
1/5 and 2/5
Comparison ownership scores 1 out of 5 and FAQ coverage scores 2 out of 5, the two lowest signals in the entire audit. The site names no competitor anywhere, and no FAQ markup was detected. Across 37 published pieces averaging 343 words, not one carries a visible publish date.
There is no "WorkRight vs Equifax" page, no "WorkRight vs Tracker I-9" page, and no structured question-and-answer content for engines to quote directly.
Why it matters
Comparison and FAQ are the two formats AI engines pull answers from most readily, and they map exactly onto the middle and bottom of the funnel. A buyer comparing you to Equifax will read a comparison page. Right now the only one available is written by someone else. Missing dates also make every post look stale to engines that weight freshness.
