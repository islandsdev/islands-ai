What we found

Six gaps, each with the fix.

What the data shows for Wellgreens, why it puts orders at risk, and which plan closes each gap. SEO means ranking in Google’s classic results. GEO and AEO mean getting quoted and linked inside AI answers.

01 · Profile namesHigh risk
10 of 10

Google Business Profiles carry keywords in the business name. Every profile is named “Wellgreens [City] Weed Cannabis Dispensary.” Google’s guidelines require the name to match your real-world storefront and rule out product, service and location details.

As shown in Google Maps: Wellgreens La Mesa - Lake Murray Rd Weed Cannabis Dispensary

Why it matters

Stuffed names often help rankings today, which is why they’re tempting to keep. But any user or competitor can suggest an edit, and Google says unnecessary information in a name can get a profile suspended. A suspension would take a profile holding thousands of reviews off the map.

How Islands closes it

Google Business Profile add-on. We confirm what each storefront sign says, rename all 10 profiles in staggered edits, keep the neighborhood in the address and on the store page, and keep every profile clean month to month.

02 · Addresses and linksFix now
3 of 10

Profiles with an address or link mismatch against your site. Kearny Mesa’s profile says “8039 Balboa Ave # A”; your homepage says “# B.” The Lake Murray profile name says “Rd”; its address and your site say “Blvd.” Vista’s website field points to thefloraverde.com, which redirects to the Vista page with no tracking tags.

Store count: your site lists 10 stores. A July 2026 Nabis feature says Wellgreens reached 12 California stores by April 2025. If two closed or were sold, their profiles and listings need to be marked closed.

Why it matters

Matching name, address and phone (NAP) across Google, your site, Weedmaps, Leafly and Yelp is a core local trust signal. And without tracking on every profile link, you can’t see how many orders start on Google Maps.

How Islands closes it

The profile add-on aligns suite numbers and street names everywhere. Plan 2 rolls out the tracked-link pattern Encinitas already uses (/locations/[store]?utm_source=gmb&utm_medium=organic-[store]) to all 10, starting with Vista.

03 · ChatGPT and structured dataBehind
7 of 16

Buyer questions where ChatGPT links to you, your lowest engine. Perplexity does on 13, Gemini on 11 and Claude on 9. Your store pages have no LocalBusiness (Store) markup and their FAQs have no FAQPage markup. Off-site there’s no Google Knowledge Panel, LinkedIn company page, Wikidata entry, Crunchbase profile, press page or named leadership, and no llms.txt file.

Conflicting facts: the site says “since 2018”; the Nabis feature says 2021. Google’s snippet still shows “Reader Best of 2024,” and Kearny Mesa claims “ranked #1 in San Diego” with no source.

Why it matters

AI engines build their picture of a business from structured data and third-party profiles. With so little of either, ChatGPT has the least to go on, the likeliest reason it trails the other engines. Engines also repeat whichever version of a fact they find first.

How Islands closes it

Plan 1 writes for the questions ChatGPT misses, starting with STIIIZY, delivery and edibles. Our keyword-gap and writing agents took Blanka from 20 to 132 AI-answer appearances in 90 days. Plan 3 adds Store and FAQPage schema, llms.txt, the entity profiles and one settled set of facts.

04 · Blog and resourcesHigh risk
25 posts

Posts targeting six cities where your site lists no store: Carlsbad has 7, Oceanside 6 and San Marcos 6, plus paired delivery posts for Del Mar, Solana Beach and Rancho Santa Fe. The resources sitemap was bulk-modified on September 14 and 15, 2026, and holds five overlapping Comic-Con posts and a typo in a live URL (…weed-delivery-in-visata-ca).

/resources/the-best-dispensary-san-diego competes with your homepage, and /resources/vista-dispensary competes with your Vista store page. Similar posts overlap Mission Valley and both La Mesa stores.

Why it matters

Google’s spam policies name near-identical city pages that funnel visitors to one destination as doorway pages. And every post chasing “best dispensary in San Diego” splits signals with the pages that actually take orders.

How Islands closes it

Plan 3 pulls Search Console data, then keeps, refreshes, merges (301 redirect) or removes each post. Plan 1 replaces the city delivery posts with one strong delivery and service-area page.

05 · Mobile speedBehind
6.9 s

For your homepage’s main content to load on a phone. Google’s Lighthouse test scores mobile performance 49 out of 100. The page is unresponsive for 1,350 ms while it loads and takes 11.3 seconds to become fully usable. Layout stability is fine (CLS 0.001).

Why it matters

Your buyers search “near me” on a phone, often on the way somewhere. Eleven seconds before the menu works is plenty of time to tap the next result.

How Islands closes it

Plan 3: compress and resize the hero and deals carousel images, defer third-party scripts (the Curator.io Instagram feed is a likely suspect), then re-test and report before and after.
