Where things stand.

The analytics foundation is rebuilt and measuring correctly. The content engine is live and publishing daily with no approval gate, and 20 posts are committed for August. A designer joins Wednesday to revamp brand, UX and CRO. The backlink programme starts now, included in the plan. The site restructure is mid-flight across seven phases through August 28.

Two things need your attention this week. First, Search Console shows organic sessions down about 66% while clicks are down about 15% since late June — a gap that large between two metrics measuring nearly the same thing usually means part of the decline is measurement, not lost traffic. We are reconciling both sources before we accept the headline number. Second, we need to confirm whether the new URL hierarchy has reached production ahead of its redirects.

The one question that gates the rest of the plan. Phase 1 of the restructure was built and signed off on staging. Evidence on the live site suggests the new /locations/ hierarchy may already be serving in production, ahead of Phase 2 internal linking and Phase 3 redirects, which are due August 14 and 15.

If that is what happened, it would explain several things at once: the template defects on lesson pages, structured data pointing at URLs that no longer resolve, and part of the traffic decline. It changes the remediation path substantially, so GOLF-77 is being answered first and everything in weeks two to four sits behind it.

On the traffic number

In July we consolidated two competing GA4 properties, cleared duplicate tags that were inflating pageviews, fixed cross-domain tracking and excluded internal test traffic. Every one of those corrections reduces the reported figure without a single visitor going anywhere.

The start date also needs pinning down: your team reports June 23, our tracking shows around June 28. Both the size and the start of the decline are under active investigation this week, and we would rather give you a number we can stand behind than a fast one.
