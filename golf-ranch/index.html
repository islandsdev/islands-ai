03 — Live defects

Eight things shipping right now.

All directly observed on the live site. Ranked by severity. The first four are hours of work.

1

Broken template tag on all five lesson pages

Every /lessons/[city] page renders a literal {location} placeholder in visible copy, with a generic sitewide title, no meta description, and no structured data. Broken text is reaching both customers and Google.

2

Empty phone and map fields in your schema

telephone and hasMap are blank on all seven location pages. This is the single biggest local-search gap: "near me" results are assembled from exactly these fields.

3

Structured data points at a URL that doesn't resolve

The JSON-LD url field references /location/ (singular) while pages serve from /locations/ (plural). You are telling engines your canonical page is a broken link. Broken markup is worse than no markup.

05 — Root causes

Five things suppressing local discovery.

1. Google cannot place your six locations

No published phone number or street address anywhere on the site or in Google's knowledge graph. Empty schema fields. No Knowledge Panel on branded search, no Wikidata record, no company LinkedIn page. For a six-site physical business this is the highest-severity structural finding in the audit, and the most plausible reason you sit at position 21 on a 60,500-a-month term you should own in six metros.

2. Structured data exists but is broken

Location pages carry SportsActivityLocation markup with its most important fields empty and its canonical URL pointing nowhere. No FAQPage schema despite genuinely good FAQ content. No Event markup despite the Golf Ranch Classic and 2v2 leagues. No Offer markup despite bucket pricing. This is the cheapest fix in the document with the widest blast radius.

07 — The strategy

Six local businesses that share a brand.

Golf Ranch is currently built as one national website that mentions six cities. It should be built as six local businesses that share a brand, a template, and a domain. This is the central recommendation of the audit.

Nobody searches for a national driving range chain. They search for somewhere to hit balls tonight, twenty minutes from where they are standing. Every ranking signal that decides that search is scored per location, not per brand. A single blended site cannot compete on any of them.

Architecture: one domain, six city hubs

/glendale-az/       ← canonical location home
  /glendale-az/lessons/    instructors, pricing, booking
  /glendale-az/clinics/    Women's 101, Men's 101, 102 series
  /glendale-az/ranch-pass/ membership, local pricing
  /glendale-az/events/    corporate, leagues, the Classic
  /glendale-az/free-bucket/local offer, local form

/grand-prairie-tx/  /richardson-tx/  /lees-summit-mo/
/shoal-creek-mo/    /brookfield-ct/

Subfolders, not subdomains, and not six separate sites. Every link, mention, and citation Golf Ranch has ever earned accrues to one domain. Six separate domains would each start from zero authority. Subfolders let Glendale inherit the authority Brookfield earned while giving each city its own indexable, locally-optimised home.

Sequencing matters

Because a restructure appears to be mid-flight already, finish and stabilise the current migration before starting this one. Two overlapping URL changes with incomplete redirects is how a site loses another 30%.
