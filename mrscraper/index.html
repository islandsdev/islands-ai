Sixty five days of content performance, read from GA4 and Google Search Console. What the blog earned, where the traffic came from, which articles put people into the signup funnel, and where MrScraper now appears inside Google’s AI Overviews.

In short

The site took 12,376 sessions. The blog accounted for 3,998 views across 744 pages and carried 1,862 key events, 17.23% of everything the property recorded. Sixteen signup funnel events trace back to a specific article. Nine of those sixteen came from four articles Islands wrote. Three Islands articles are now cited or ranked on the first page of Google, two of them inside the AI Overview.

06

What needs qualifying

Four things in this data should be settled before any of it is used to make a decision.

Key events need a definition

  • The property records 10,809 key events across the period, 1,862 of them on blog pages.
  • Weekly pulls taken from 20 September onward returned zero key events across every channel.
  • Both readings come from the same property, so the period total is concentrated in the earlier weeks.
  • The event definition should be confirmed before key events are used as a performance measure.

Signup funnel is not signups

  • The exploration counts page views on /auth/signup, /auth/register, /auth/google/success and /auth/email-verification.
  • Each is attributed to the blog article the visitor landed on first.
  • It shows people reaching the signup funnel. It does not confirm completed accounts.

Windows must match

  • The same three month Search Console view read 2.04K clicks and 694K impressions on 16 October, and 1.36K and 452K ten days later, because higher volume July days rolled out of range.
  • GA4 figures on this page cover 17 August to 20 October. Ahrefs figures cover the seven days to 28 October. They are not comparable to each other.

Ahrefs traffic analytics is a different measure

  • The Ahrefs beta reports 11.5K visits and 158K page views for the week to 28 October.
  • That figure covers the whole domain including app.mrscraper.com, the logged in product.
  • It is not comparable to the GA4 website property or to organic traffic, and is excluded from the numbers above.
