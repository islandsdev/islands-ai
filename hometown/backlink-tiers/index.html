How it works

What you are buying

The placement. Each placement is a new article, written for the site and approved by us before it goes live, containing one dofollow link to the Hometown page shown under each site. None of these publishers label the article as sponsored. The article’s own address exists only once it is published, and is reported back on delivery.

The prices. Fixed buy-now prices, not bids, and all in: publisher, platform fee and the article, quoted live on 21 October 2026 at 1,200 words. GetReach bills in euros; USD figures use the ECB reference rate of 16 October 2026, €1 = $1.1253. Marketplace prices, fees and the exchange rate move day to day, so they are re-checked before any order is placed. Nothing here has been purchased.

Prepared by Islands. Scored with our backlink tier method: hard quality gates first, then relevance, authority, referring domains and traffic.

The columns. Domain Rating is Ahrefs’ 0 to 100 measure of a site’s backlink strength. Relevance is how closely the site’s readers match yours, and Score blends relevance, authority, referring domains and traffic; both are out of 100. Borderline marks a score within three points of a tier boundary, where a fraction of a point decides the tier.
