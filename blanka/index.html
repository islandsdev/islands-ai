Why

Two findings shaped every decision

Before writing a single subject line, the plan was built against what the data from Blanka's HubSpot and the competitor gap analysis actually says. These two findings drove the rest.

1. Deliverability is the gating issue

  • Prospect email open rates dropped from 8.0% in July to 3.4% in September when the sending list tripled from ~5K to ~16K recipients.
  • No content rewrite fixes this. Fixing deliverability (list hygiene, suppression, re-engagement, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) is the biggest Q4 lever.
  • This content plan assumes a parallel deliverability effort. Even the best newsletter cannot out-write a 3% open rate.

2. Specific patterns still win inside the current list

  • Even on the broken list, some emails clear 5-6% open rate against the 3.6% site average.
  • Common thread: specific "you" hooks addressing reader identity, real founder case studies, career-pivoter reframes, medical beauty angles.
  • The November plan leans into these patterns and drops the weaker ones (generic category plays, abstract trend explainers).
