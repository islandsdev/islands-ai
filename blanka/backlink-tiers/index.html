How to read this

Prices are fixed buy-now prices, not bids, and are all-in: publisher cost, platform fee and the article, quoted live today at 1,200 words. GetReach bills in euros; dollar figures use today’s rate of €1 = $1.1222. Marketplace prices, platform fees and the exchange rate all move day to day, so we re-check them before any order is placed. Nothing here has been purchased.

Each placement is a new article, written for the site and approved by us before it goes live, containing one dofollow link to the Blanka page shown under each site. None of these publishers label the article as sponsored. The article’s own address exists only once it is published, and is reported back on delivery.

Domain Rating is Ahrefs’ 0 to 100 measure of a site’s backlink strength. Relevance and score are both out of 100. “Borderline” marks a score within three points of a tier boundary, where the tier rests on a fraction of a point and deserves a human read rather than a confident label.
