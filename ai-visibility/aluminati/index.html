04 · Entity footprintHigh risk
No knowledge panel

Google returns no Knowledge Panel on your brand search, no LinkedIn company page surfaces on it, no Crunchbase profile appears in the knowledge graph, and no named founder or leadership appears in public sources. The closest Wikipedia match is the article for aluminium, the metal, and the Wikidata record that resolves is a 2018 scientific paper, not the company.

Entity clarity scores 5 of 5 on your own site, and the off-site record scores Behind on 4 of 8 signals. Your site is clear about who you are. The web around it is not.

Why it matters

Engines decide what a brand is from structured records and third-party profiles, not from the site alone. When they cannot resolve Aluminati as a company, they fall back on the directories that are easy to resolve. This is the likeliest single reason ChatGPT cites you zero times.

How Islands closes it

Plan 2. We claim and connect the entity records, LinkedIn, Crunchbase and Wikidata, publish a named leadership and about page, and keep one consistent set of facts across every profile so the engines stop guessing.
