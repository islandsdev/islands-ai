Ablefs performance reportSeptember 2026

TL;DR

September hit 2 of 4 targets: AI citations and Authority Score landed, while organic keywords and traffic grew but fell short.

  • AI search is the standout. Cited pages rose from 9 to 19 (target 15+), and mentions now appear on three engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, AI Mode), up from Gemini only.
  • Rankings and traffic moved, but not enough. Keywords rose 291 to 305 (target ~360) and estimated organic traffic 32 to 37 (target ~50).
  • Google sees us but rarely clicks. 7.66K impressions over 28 days turned into 31 clicks (0.4% CTR, average position 45.9).
  • The main blocker is unchanged. ~227 noindexed location/service pages and the unsubmitted disavow are still holding back keyword and traffic growth.

Blog performance

Blog pages brought in 137 views from 62 active users in September, which is 20.6% of all site views.

  • 43 blog URLs got traffic. Average engagement was 20 seconds, and each user viewed 2.2 pages on average.
  • The /blog index was the top page, with 32 views from 6 users (33s engagement). A few readers are browsing several posts in one visit.
  • Top posts by views: Modern Office Cleaning Services Checklist, Post-Construction Cleaning Punch List, Day Porter Services for Schools, and Green Commercial Cleaning with Hospital-Grade standards.
  • Views peaked on Sep 4 (about 20 views) and then held steady at 3 to 10 a day.

Source: GA4 Pages and screens, filtered to "blog", Sep 1–30, 2026.

Search performance

Google showed ablefs.com 7.66K times in the last 28 days, but only 31 of those turned into clicks.

Metric (GSC, last 28 days) Value
Clicks 31
Impressions 7.66K
Average CTR 0.4%
Average position 45.9

An average position of 45.9 puts most listings around page 5, where they get seen and almost never clicked. Semrush agrees: 99.3% of ranking positions are plain organic results, and we have no AI Overview placements. There are two ways to grow clicks. One is to move page-2 keywords (positions 11–20) onto page 1. The other is to unlock the noindexed location pages so Google can rank them.

Sources: Google Search Console Performance (Web), Aug 31–Sep 27, 2026; Semrush SERP distribution, Sep 30, 2026.
