Progress report · July to October 2026

Four months of content, and a new foothold in AI search

What Islands built for Able Facility Solutions since July 1: a focused library of 72 guides, a growing keyword base, and a site that went from invisible to cited across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google’s AI Mode.

In short

Able’s Insights blog went from a dozen scattered posts to 72 guides, three a week without a miss. Organic keywords grew from 166 to a September peak of 312, Authority Score moved 12 to 13, and AI search visibility rose from zero to a standing presence. Clicks and non-branded rankings have not caught up yet, because most new rankings sit on page two or three. Turning that visibility into page-one traffic is the work for the next quarter.

The build

A content engine that covers Able’s real verticals, end to end

The library now speaks to the people who choose cleaning vendors across every setting Able serves: offices, healthcare and hospitals, K-12 and universities, industrial and manufacturing, banking, fitness, auto dealerships, government, hospitality, retail, and restaurants. Every guide targets facility managers and operations directors. The off-ICP consumer articles that predated the program were dropped.

Search Console connected

Modeled organic traffic has held roughly flat while the keyword base grew. Search Console, now connected, shows the same shape: rising impressions at an average position around 21. Non-branded commercial terms, variations of “commercial cleaning,” are not yet on page one. Moving them up is the main job for Q4, and it depends on strengthening Able’s location and service pages more than on publishing more articles. Referring domains and backlinks are shown through September: a sharp October jump in both is a likely low-quality influx we are cleaning up, covered under What’s next.

What’s next

Four priorities for the next quarter

The content engine is healthy. The next gains come from site health and from converting visibility into page-one traffic.

Priority 01

Clean the backlink profile

Referring domains spiked sharply in October with no matching authority gain, a pattern that usually signals a low-quality or spam influx. We are running a backlink audit and will submit a disavow to protect the domain.

Priority 02

Strengthen location and service pages

These are the reason non-branded commercial terms are not yet on page one. The highest-leverage work for traffic.

Priority 03

Internal-linking and on-page pass

Route authority to the pages ranking on page two or three and push them onto page one.

Priority 04

Consolidate overlapping guides

At 72 posts, a few topics now overlap. We consolidate and refresh rather than add near-duplicates.
