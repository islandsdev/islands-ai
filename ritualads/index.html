What Claude thinks you are

"What is Ritual Ads?" (asked of the AIs)

Ritual Ads calls itself the world's first Cinematic Intelligence Studio: a human-directed, AI-native creative-as-a-service company powered by its proprietary Ritual Engine. It bridges high-end film production and generative AI, building adaptive pipelines that learn a brand's decision logic and performance signals to increase creative velocity without losing integrity. There are three ways in: fully produced campaigns, hands-on AI consulting, and custom Creative Engine (Large Brand Model) deployments. The proof is real and citable: 3,200+ videos produced by the engine, a Martha Stewart campaign that drove 1.5 million daily impressions with zero practical sets, a Reebok Angel Reese campaign that sold out within hours, and the ability to rebuild one cut into twelve on-brand variations under a single approval trail. Work spans Reebok, Pizza Hut, Martha Stewart and Super Bowl pre-visualization.

Here's the disconnect. Your foundation is strong: a crystal-clear entity (a 'Cinematic Intelligence Studio'), wide schema coverage, top-tier E-E-A-T from Reebok, Martha Stewart and Super Bowl work, fresh cadence, and hard, quotable proof like '3,200+ videos' and '1.5 million daily impressions.' But when we asked the AIs the questions your buyers actually ask (best AI ad creative agency, best AI video production studio for advertising, best generative AI creative agency for enterprise brands), Ritual Ads never came back. Every answer went to Superside, Monks, Silverside AI, Secret Level and Synima, and Google's own AI Overview named AirPost, Runway, Adobe Firefly and Monks while skipping you. Two fixable things are driving it. First, there is no llms.txt telling AI crawlers who to trust, so the models have no clean signal to pull from. Second, this category is being defined right now by comparison and 'best X for Y' content, and you own almost none of it (your comparison coverage is the weakest signal on your site), because the site leans on proprietary terminology like 'Ritual Engine' and 'Large Brand Model' rather than the plain-language questions buyers type. Whoever publishes that answer-shaped content first becomes the default, and today that is not you.
