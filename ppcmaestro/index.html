What Claude thinks you are

"What is PPC Maestro?" (asked of the AIs)

PPC Maestro is a profit-first Amazon PPC agency built by an engineer, Bernard Nader, out of Miami. Instead of chasing vanity metrics like impressions and top-line revenue, it runs a proprietary 'Profit Feedback Loop' that finds and cuts the 'bleeders,' the ads that drain budget without converting, to pull down ACoS and TACoS and scale 7-figure brands on actual margin. It is a deliberately boutique team, an Amazon Verified Partner, month-to-month with no long-term lock-in, and it goes deep on the granular work that wins in high-competition categories like supplements and beauty: premium listings, ingredient-level keyword research, and strategic launches.

Here's the disconnect. Your foundation is genuinely strong: 78 pages, deep SOP hubs like 'Cut Bleeders' and 'Reduce High ACoS,' agency-vs-in-house comparison pages, an Amazon Verified Partner badge, and real testimonials from 7-figure CEOs. AI can even identify you by name. But when we asked the AIs the questions your buyers actually ask (best Amazon PPC agency for 7-figure sellers, for supplement brands, to cut ACoS), PPC Maestro did not come back. The answers went to Olifant Digital, IG PPC, Canopy Management, Ad Badger and Trivium Group. Even on 'best boutique agency,' the AIs named profit-first rivals like Olifant Digital and PPC Jumpstart ahead of you. An independent probe (DataForSEO, Sept 12, 16 prompts across 5 engines) found the same: cited just 3 of 80 times, and placed in Google's top 30 on 0 of 19 tracked terms. The reason is simple and fixable: you have the blogs but stopped publishing months ago, there's no schema, and no fresh answer-shaped content, so there is nothing recent for Google or the LLMs to rank or quote. And 'the Amazon PPC agency ChatGPT recommends' is still up for grabs. Whoever publishes the trustworthy, answer-shaped content first becomes the default.
