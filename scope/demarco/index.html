More leads coming in. More follow-ups going out automatically. More deals in the pipeline, without adding headcount. Here is what we are thinking, and how we get there.

The challenge

Growing a roofing business without a system is an uphill battle.

Most roofing businesses at the early growth stage are entirely dependent on referrals and word of mouth. There is no repeatable way to find new customers, no automated follow-up when someone shows interest, and no visibility into where deals stand or why they stall.

The result: a lot of potential revenue that never converts, and a sales process that lives entirely in someone's head or phone. Growth is possible, but it is inconsistent and hard to scale.

What we solve

A predictable, automated sales operation. Built once, runs continuously.

  • More inbound leads from territory-specific outreach targeting the right homeowners and businesses in your area
  • Faster response times with automated follow-up the moment a lead fills a form or shows interest
  • More deals closed through a structured pipeline that tracks every opportunity and keeps nothing from slipping through
  • Less manual work with outreach sequences, follow-ups, and reminders running automatically in the background

01 — Suggested

Website Audit + Conversion Improvements

Your website is likely getting visitors that are not converting into leads. Before we turn on any outbound engine, we want to make sure the destination works. We will audit the existing site, identify the gaps, and recommend concrete changes to copy, forms, and calls to action that turn more visitors into actual inquiries.

  • Full review of current site structure and conversion flow
  • ICP definition: which job types, territories, and customer profiles to prioritize (residential, commercial, insurance claims, etc.)
  • Specific recommendations for form placement, messaging, and page structure
  • Implementation of priority changes ahead of outbound launch

02 — Suggested

Territory Mapping + Lead Database

Instead of waiting for leads to find you, we build a database of the right prospects in your target area and go to them. We pull from Google Maps, local directories, property records, and other sources to build a list that is scored, prioritized, and ready to contact, organized by neighborhood, property type, and estimated job value.

  • Geographic territory definition based on Derek's service area and growth goals
  • Lead sourcing from multiple data sources (homeowners, property managers, commercial addresses)
  • Scoring and prioritization by proximity, property type, and job-type fit
  • Continuously refreshable as the business expands into new areas or job categories
Scope to be refined on call
03 — Suggested

Automated Outbound Outreach

We build and run outreach sequences to sourced leads via email and SMS. Messages are personalized by lead type so a residential homeowner gets different copy than a commercial property manager or an insurance adjuster. Multi-touch sequences mean no lead goes cold after one unanswered message, and we test and improve messaging over time based on what gets replies.

  • Outreach sequences built for each lead segment (residential, commercial, insurance)
  • Automated multi-touch follow-up cadence: email, SMS, or both depending on what performs
  • Messaging written by Islands, reviewed with Derek before launch
  • A/B testing of subject lines and message content to improve reply rates over time
  • Warm replies routed immediately to Derek for manual follow-up
Scope to be refined on call
04 — Suggested

Inbound Lead Nurture

Right now, anyone who fills out a form on your site probably waits hours (or longer) for a response. Speed matters: the first business to respond wins the job more often than not. We build automation that responds to new form submissions within minutes, confirms interest, and starts a follow-up sequence for leads who go quiet after the first reply.

  • Automatic email and/or SMS sent to every form submission within minutes
  • Hot leads (high intent) flagged and routed to Derek immediately
  • Multi-step follow-up sequence for leads that do not respond to the first message
  • Unsubscribe and opt-out handling built in
Scope to be refined on call
05 — Suggested

CRM + Pipeline Setup

Without a CRM, deals live in text threads and memory. We set up a lightweight pipeline tracker so every lead has a status, every follow-up has a due date, and nothing disappears. We will recommend a tool based on what Derek already uses (or prefers) and configure it so it is actually something the team will use, not just another thing to ignore.

  • CRM tool selection based on Derek's existing stack and preferences (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, or similar)
  • Pipeline stages configured to match Derek's actual sales process
  • Integration with inbound and outbound systems so leads flow in automatically
  • Simple reporting: how many leads, where they stall, what is closing
  • Training so the team can manage and update it without our help day-to-day
